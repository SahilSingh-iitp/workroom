DELIVER: prototype or process redesign + 3 student reactions

Why WORKROOM exists

Talent is everywhere.
Opportunity to practise isn't.

Not everyone has a campus full of active clubs, builders, mentors or industry projects. WORKROOM is a small step toward making practical experience easier to access — wherever you study.

  • Open to every course and college
  • Real problems, not textbook exercises
  • Individual and team formats
  • Evidence over empty certificates
  • Feedback that helps you improve
